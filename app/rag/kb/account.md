# Account

## Cancel subscription
You can cancel anytime under Settings, Subscription, Cancel plan. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. Cancelling does not trigger a refund for the current period unless it is within the 14 day refund window.

## Change plan
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## Reset password
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed and expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive it, check spam or contact support.

## Two-factor authentication
Enable two-factor authentication under Settings, Security. We support authenticator apps and SMS codes. Lost your device? Support can verify your identity and reset two-factor.

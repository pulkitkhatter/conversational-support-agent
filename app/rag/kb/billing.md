# Billing

## Refund policy
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of a charge. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full once verified by the billing team, usually within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two charges for the same amount in the same billing period, we treat it as a duplicate charge. Support will open a billing investigation ticket and the billing team reviews it within 2 business days.

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices are available under Settings, Billing, Invoices. A receipt is emailed after every successful payment. You can update the billing email at any time.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Update your card under Settings, Billing, Payment method. Failed payments are retried 3 times over 7 days.
